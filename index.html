What this computer needs to record and run tests. Everything is free and runs locally. Your APKs, flows and data never leave this PC.

Checklist

Android tools without Android Studio

Installs only what Understudy needs from Google's official server: adb, the emulator and one light system image, then creates a virtual device called Understudy_Pixel (2 GB RAM). About 1.5 GB to download. A window shows the progress. Android Studio is not needed and can be uninstalled.

Typing non-English text

English text works out of the box. To type Bengali or other scripts, install the free, open-source ADB Keyboard app on the device once (add its APK on the Record tab and press Install). Understudy switches to it only while typing and switches back afterwards.
